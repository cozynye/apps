Nuni is a mood diary that takes 10 seconds a day.

Pick a cloud that matches your mood, tap what you did, add a line if you like. That's your entry. Over time, Nuni's calendar fills with little clouds and you start to see your patterns.

HOW IT WORKS
• Five moods, five clouds — from rainy to sunny
• 20 activity tags, plus your own
• A one-line note and a photo, if you want
• Monthly calendar and streak counter
• One gentle reminder a day, at the time you choose
• Face ID lock

NUNI PREMIUM
• See which activities lift your mood
• Yearly trends and your "year in clouds"
• iCloud backup and restore
• All home screen widgets and themes
• Unlimited photos per entry

YOUR DIARY STAYS YOURS
Entries are stored on your device. No account, no sign-up. We never see your diary.
