Support

Nuni (mood diary) and Fourpop (photo booth) are made by Chagyung, an independent developer.

Contact

Email nunicozy@gmail.com. Please include the app name, your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Restore purchases

There are no accounts. After reinstalling or on a new device, open the app and tap Settings › Restore purchases (also on the Premium screen) while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad.

Your data

Diary entries and photo strips stay on your device (and in your own iCloud if you turn on backup). See the Privacy Policy.
