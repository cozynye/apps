Privacy Policy

Effective: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what Nuni and Fourpop (each an "App"), published by Chagyung ("we"), do with information. The short version: your diary and photos stay on your device; we only receive anonymous usage statistics and crash reports.

1. What stays on your device

  • Nuni: mood entries, tags, notes, photos, streaks, settings.
  • Fourpop: captured photos, finished photo strips, downloaded frame and sticker packs, settings.

This content is stored only on your device. If you turn on iCloud backup (Nuni), it is copied to your own iCloud account under Apple's privacy terms. We never receive it and cannot read it.

2. What we collect

Data Purpose Service Linked to you?
App usage events (screens opened, features used, such as "entry created" or "strip shared"), device model, iOS version, app version, language, country Understand which features are used and fix problems Google Firebase Analytics No. An anonymous installation ID only
Crash reports (stack trace, device state at crash) Fix crashes Google Firebase Crashlytics No
Frame and sticker pack downloads (which pack, when, IP address in server logs) Deliver content Cloudflare No

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, or the content of your diary or photos. Analytics events never contain diary text, tag names you typed, or images.

3. Why we are allowed to process this (legal basis)

We process usage and crash data to run and improve the Apps (legitimate interest). Where your country's law requires consent for analytics, we ask for it on first launch and you can withdraw it in Settings › Privacy at any time.

4. Who receives data

Apple (App Store, iCloud), Google (Firebase) and Cloudflare act as our processors and only use data to provide their services. Data may be stored on servers outside Vietnam and Thailand (United States, EU). We rely on these providers' standard data protection terms for such transfers. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

5. How long we keep data

Usage events and crash reports: up to 14 months, then deleted or aggregated.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Vietnam's Decree 13/2023 on personal data protection and Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because the data we hold is anonymous, we usually cannot identify which records are yours; if you send us your anonymous installation ID (Settings › Privacy › Copy ID) we will delete the related analytics and crash data within 30 days. You can delete everything on your device from Settings › Delete all data, and export your Nuni entries from Settings › Export.

7. Children

The Apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents who believe a child has used an App can contact us.

8. Security

Content on your device is protected by iOS device encryption; Nuni offers an additional Face ID / passcode lock. Data sent to our providers is encrypted in transit (TLS).

9. Changes

We will show material changes in the App before they take effect and update the date above.
